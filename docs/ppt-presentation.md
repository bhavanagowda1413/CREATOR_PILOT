# Hackathon Presentation Slides - CreatorPilot

## SLIDE 1: Cover
**CreatorPilot**
*Autonomous AI Content Growth Agent*
24-Hour AI + n8n Hackathon MVP

---

## SLIDE 2: Problem & Solution
**Problem:**
"Content creators spend a lot of time manually researching trends, analyzing previous posts, understanding audience engagement, deciding what to post, writing captions and hooks, selecting hashtags, and choosing the best time to publish. This information is scattered across multiple sources, making content planning repetitive, time-consuming, and difficult to optimize consistently."

**Solution:**
"CreatorPilot is an AI-powered autonomous content growth agent built with n8n. It analyzes creator context, previous content performance, audience signals and trend information to intelligently decide what content should be created next. It then generates the content package, recommends or schedules publishing, monitors performance, and learns from the results to improve future content strategies."

---

## SLIDE 3: Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4 + Lucide Icons + Recharts
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express + TypeScript + SQLite Database Engine
- **AI Engine**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash + Intelligent DEMO_MODE Fallback
- **Automation**: n8n Webhook Integration Pipelines

---

## SLIDE 4: Autonomous Growth Cycle Flow
```
Creator Profile
     ↓
Performance Data & Signals
     ↓
n8n Webhook Collection
     ↓
AI Signal Analysis
     ↓
Autonomous Decision (Topic, Format, Time, Priority)
     ↓
Structured Content Generation (Hook, Script, Caption, Visuals)
     ↓
Scheduled Publishing
     ↓
Performance Monitoring
     ↓
AI Learning Loop & Strategy Update
```

---

## SLIDE 5: Core Differentiators & Future Scope
- **Autonomous Decision Engine**: Doesn't just generate text — decides *what* to create next.
- **Continuous AI Feedback Loop**: Automatically turns past views & saves into future strategy directives.
- **Decoupled Fallback Mode**: Works seamlessly online or in 100% demo presentation mode.
- **Future Scope**: Multi-platform direct publishing, live audience comment sentiment analysis, auto-video editing integration.
